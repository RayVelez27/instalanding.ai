16 · faq

FAQ

A sticky head beside native details rows: the question in 600 with a ring button that fills on hover and turns its plus into a minus when open, under an ink rule.

Questions, answered

Can't find yours? Message us in the app. A real person replies within the hour, every day.

Talk to a person
Do I need to install anything?

No. The app works on its own from photos and messages. Home Plus sensors plug into an outlet or sit on the floor, and they're set up in about ten minutes with no tools.

How are the pros vetted?

Every pro is licensed, insured and background-checked, and we review their work after every job. Fewer than one in five applicants make it onto the network.

What if the AI gets the diagnosis wrong?

The pro confirms on site before any work starts. If the job turns out different, you see the new price and approve it first. You never pay for a fix you didn't need.

What happens to my home's data?

It's encrypted, it's yours, and we never sell it. You can export your full home record or delete everything from settings at any time.

Does it work for renters?

Yes. Renters can share issues straight to their landlord or property manager, with photos and a suggested fix, so things get sorted faster.
