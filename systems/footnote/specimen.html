08 · steps

Numbered steps

Paper cards with a decimal-leading-zero counter, each laid at its own slight angle (--r).

Send both halves

The answer and the spans you retrieved for it. Footnote does not need your prompt, your weights or your vector store.

Get claims back

The answer is split into assertions, each with a verdict, a confidence and the span offsets it was checked against.

Mark it up, or gate on it

Render the marks for a human, or fail the response in CI when anything comes back unsupported. Same payload either way.

The payload

Plain JSON, with the pencil marks left in.

Each claim comes back with its verdict, a confidence, and character offsets into the span it was checked against — the spans you sent, not a copy we made.

11 · faq

Margin notes

Each question is its own sheet with a red margin rule, a pencilled question mark and a + that turns as it opens. Native details/summary.

Does it need my prompt, or my model?+

No. It reads two things: the answer, and the spans you retrieved for it. Whatever produced them is your business.

What counts as a claim?+

Anything that could be true or false on its own. A sentence with three assertions comes back as three claims, each with its own verdict.

Can it be wrong?+

Yes, and it says how sure it is. Stretched exists because the line between supported and not is sometimes genuinely fuzzy; better to show you the fuzz than hide it in a score.

Does it keep my documents?+

This is a demo of an invented product, so it has nothing to keep. A real one should answer this question in its first paragraph, not its last.
