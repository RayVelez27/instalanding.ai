05 · art

Generative art

Every picture is painted in code: a canvas with class ag-art names its kind (data-ag-art: silk, chrome, contour, orb, bust, ridge, petal, flow), its palette (data-ag-pal: night, bone, ember, violet, lime, ash) and a seed; the same seed always paints the same image. Raster kinds compute at a fixed pixel budget and upscale; canvases paint lazily near the viewport, a slice per frame, and fade in. Below, one silk swatch per palette.
