Pixel night arcade

Press start.

A dark arcade language for games, launches and playful AI products: a night sky of drifting pixel stars and scanlines, Jersey 10 pixel type with a hard black step, one cyan glow, mono labels, keycaps with a key hint, and a Night / Dawn / Day switch that retints every glow. Every picture is drawn pixel by pixel in code. Extracted from the Cyber Bird arcade page.

01 · sky

Night sky & scanlines

The backdrop every part sits on: near-black --cb-bg, a canvas of square pixel stars that drift sideways, twinkle and parallax with scroll (tinted to the theme), an aura of three radial glows mixed from --cb-glow, --cb-glow2 and --cb-accent, and 1px scanlines every 3px. The page fixes all three to the viewport; any ancestor with a transform contains them, as here.

02 · nav

Nav & sky switch

A frosted bar (--cb-glass over --cb-blur, 14px corners, a hairline): the pixel-drawn cyber-pickup beside the CYBER BIRD wordmark in Jersey 10 with BIRD in cyan, muted links that light on hover, the Night / Dawn / Day segmented switch (mono capitals; the pressed one inverts to ink) and a pixel GitHub icon key. The switch is live: it sets data-cb-theme on the .cb root. Under 900px the links go.

03 · buttons

Keycaps, buttons & chips

Buttons are 12px-radius slabs in Geist 600: cyan with a top highlight and a cyan under-glow, or a hairline ghost; both lift 2px on hover, and the primary carries a mono key hint (SPACE) in a 1px outlined keycap. Keycaps proper are Jersey 10 on --cb-key in a 3px ink border with a hard 4px black step. Around them: the blinking square-dot eyebrow pill, mono chips with an ink figure, the segmented switch, pixel icon keys, the fullscreen key and an underlined link button.

04 · type

Pixel display type

Display is Jersey 10, a pixel face, always weight 400. The title runs at --cb-title (line-height .78) in --cb-title-ink with a hard black step (--cb-step-title) and a glow mixed from --cb-glow; a smaller word rides its corner as a tilted sticker in a cyan-to-blue gradient with its own black step. Section heads stack a cyan mono kicker behind a 24px rule, an --cb-h2 title with a --cb-step shadow whose second line drops to --cb-dim, and a muted line.

07 · bento

Feature bento

A 6-column bento of glass cards (18px corners, a hairline, a faint top-lit gradient) with a spotlight mixed from --cb-glow2 that follows the pointer. Each card pairs a dim mono tag, a Jersey 10 title and a muted line with a live pixel picture: the cyber-pickup hovering on its jets over speed lines, a steel rocket stack it threads, three sky swatches that switch the theme, a charge gauge with a cell ladder, and a code well.

Pilot notes
  1. Short taps beat panic. One boost lifts you about a truck-length — chain them, don't spam them.
  2. Aim for the middle. The next gap can only drift so far, so centered flight leaves room either way.
  3. Watch the nose. The truck tilts with your velocity — when it dips past level, it's time.
  4. Ten is a battery. Every 10 points charges 25%. Forty is a full pack.
