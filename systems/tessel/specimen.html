05 · blocks

Isometric blocks

Everything illustrated is generated isometric SVG: P(x, y, z) projects a point, box() draws a block with three lit faces (each a generated gradient), a white specular edge and a contact shadow, and plane matrices lay flat drawings onto floors and walls. The scene is the page's Scale platform: a server grid with blinking green LEDs and gold vents, flowing gold and periwinkle wiring on the deck, an orbiting satellite, live region pills and the floor label. Below, the four face ramps (navy, white, periwinkle, gold).

Pixel guide

A 16 x 24 pixel-art character drawn from strings (one letter per palette colour, crisp edges, 4px per pixel), who blinks, carries a pixel speech bubble and walks on four frames (s0, s1, s0, s2) with a one-step bob. It is the page's narrator: one per page, never decoration.
