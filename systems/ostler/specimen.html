03 · type

Fill-type, headings & labels

Display words are Gabarito 900 at line-height .8 and -0.045em, sized to fill their box exactly: put the word in a .os-fillbox (a container) and give it .os-display.os-fill and --os-len, its letter count; font-size is 100cqi / (--os-len x --os-k). Its letters stand slightly tilted, each at its own angle (the page's seeded stance). Headings are Gabarito 800 (.os-h2, .os-h3), copy is Instrument Sans (.os-lead at 500, .os-muted for the quieter line) and labels are small caps-spaced (.os-eyebrow).

15 · faq

FAQ rows

A cream card: heading and muted note beside details/summary rows under hairlines, the question in Gabarito 800 (wavy underline on hover) and a round plus that turns into a minus on --os-spring; the icon wobbles on hover.

Asked in every demo

Anything else? Ask us on a call, or write and a person will answer within one working day.

Does it replace my front desk team?

No. It takes the repetitive questions so your team has time for the guests standing in front of them. Anything sensitive, unusual or upset goes to a person, with the full conversation attached.

What happens when it doesn't know?

It says so, tells the guest someone will reply shortly, and hands the thread to your team. Ostler only answers from your hotel's approved knowledge, never from guesswork.

Which systems do you connect to?

Most widely used property management systems, booking engines, POS platforms and door-lock providers. If yours isn't covered yet, we build the connector during onboarding at no extra cost.

Where is guest data stored?

In the EU, encrypted at rest and in transit. We sign a data processing agreement with every hotel and never use your guests' data to train models for anyone else.

How long does setup take?

Fourteen days on average, from signing to the first guest conversation. Expect about four hours of your team's time across that fortnight.

Can we cancel?

Monthly plans cancel with thirty days' notice. You keep a full export of every conversation, and your knowledge base, either way.
