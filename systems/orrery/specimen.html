06 · method

Method steps

Five stages that rise in a staircase, each with a rule and tick, a duration in wide type, a caps title and a line of text.

How an answer gets made

Every response passes through the same five stages. Nothing reaches you until each one is done.

  • 0.2 s

    Parse the ask

    Orrery restates your question, flags anything ambiguous, and decides how hard it needs to think.

  • 1–4 s

    Evidence first

    It searches your sources before it writes a word. The documents decide whether there is an answer.

  • Up to 8 min

    Reason in the open

    Hypotheses are drafted, tested and thrown away. Watch the working, or skip straight to the end.

  • Every claim

    Verify twice

    Each statement is checked against at least two independent passages. Anything unsupported is cut or flagged.

  • After

    Cite, then stay

    Answers ship with their sources. Change a document and Orrery tells you which answers it touched.
