01 · buttons

Buttons & fact pills

Buttons are ink-bordered blocks at --oi-radius-md with a hard --oi-shadow-btn (4px 4px 0 ink, no blur): Josefin 700, cream by default, red for the main action. Hover nudges them up-left onto --oi-shadow-lift; pressing pushes them down-right onto --oi-shadow-press, like a vending-machine key. Fact pills are outlined: a 2px ink border on cream, fully round. Hints are small copy in --oi-ink-muted.

  • 6.00 a pack
  • Up to 10 packs per order

05 · cabinet

Vending-machine cabinet

The red cabinet shell: 3px ink border, round top and tighter base (--oi-radius-cabinet over --oi-radius-base) and a 10px hard --oi-shadow-cabinet. A cap line (Vending machine · 自動販売機), a black bezel around the screen, the pickup slot (取出口) beside a yolk coin slot, and two feet. The screen is an art slot at 9:16: put your own canvas, video or image in it. Its height follows --oi-cab-h (min(80vh, 840px)); width is half of that.

06 · flavors

Flavor picker & card

Six pack buttons (aria-pressed) over a flavor card: choosing a pack sets --oi-flavor for the section, so the card's hard shadow and the Japanese name's 5px underline recolour, and the name, Japanese name and filling update. Packs get a --oi-tan-2 ground on hover and an ink border when chosen; each pack's art slot is tinted with its flavor. Six columns, three under 1080px, two under 520px.

Six fillings, one crunchy waffle

Every Oishi is the same three layers: a crisp waffle shell, soft ice cream, and a syrup center. Only the filling changes.

07 · steps

Numbered steps

Three steps under 3px ink rules: a 46px round ink counter in Josefin (the last one red), an h3, a short line and the step's Japanese label in Noto Sans JP 900. They stack under 820px.

How the machine works

Three taps from hungry to holding one.

  1. Pick a flavor

    Tap a pack on the screen. The arrows flip through all six without going back.

    味を選ぶ

  2. Choose how many

    Plus and minus set the quantity. The total updates as you go, up to 10 packs.

    数を選ぶ

  3. Tap Get!

    Your order drops into the tray. Then tap Here! to play Guezzilla for one more.

    得る
