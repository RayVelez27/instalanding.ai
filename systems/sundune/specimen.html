04 · hero

Desert sky hero

A burnt-orange to apricot sky (--sd-sky-1 to --sd-sky-3) with a pale sun disc that breathes on a 12s loop, drifting clouds, layered ochre ridges and acacias, a huge Archivo Black word rising behind four silhouette figures, a flock crossing, reeds and grass swaying and a linen grain over all. 06 · approach

Manifesto & principles

An accent label over a 21em serif statement whose words light up from 17% as you read down, then three principles over a hairline: an accent line icon, a Jost 600 title and a soft line, divided by vertical rules.

Our approach

We don’t stage culture. Every Sundune gathering is co-designed with Maasai community hosts, who set the terms, lead the ceremonies and are paid as partners, not performers.

  • Community-led

    Elders and hosts shape every programme and can say no to anything in it.

  • A fair share

    A fixed 30% of every fee goes straight to the host community’s own fund.

  • Light on the land

    Local suppliers, no single-use plastic, and every site left the way we found it.

07 · experiences

Experience rows & art frame

Ruled rows with big serif titles: the active one turns accent and grows a shuka-textured swatch before its title, with meta set right. Beside them a sticky 4:5 frame (3px radius, a deep two-layer shadow) cross-fades and settles its art as you hover or scroll: a bead collar on leather, first light on the ridges, two linked bangles on a check cloth, a long gala table to the fire at dusk, every bead, star and bulb placed by the component script. On phones each row carries its own copy of the art.

What we host

Four kinds of gathering, each designed with our community hosts around story, song, food and the land it happens on.

  • Cultural festivals

    Open-air days of music, beadwork, storytelling and food, led by the people whose culture it is.

    200 to 2,000 guests
    One to three days

  • Leadership retreats

    Three days on the plains for teams who want to slow down, listen to elders and talk about what lasts.

    12 to 60 guests
    Two or three nights

  • Weddings and ceremonies

    Celebrations that honour both families, with blessings, song and dress planned together with you.

    40 to 300 guests
    One or two days

  • Sunset galas

    Long tables under acacias, a choir at dusk and a fire that keeps going after the speeches stop.

    80 to 500 guests
    One evening

Process steps
