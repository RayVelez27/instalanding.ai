12 · faq

FAQ

A staggered display title beside native details rows on graphite rules; the pill dash at the end of each question shrinks and slides when it opens.

Asked at the intake desk

Before you ask.

Does equipment need to be decontaminated first?

Yes. Clean and disinfect it under your facility's protocol and attach the decontamination tag. Anything that arrives without one is bagged and cleaned before it reaches the bench, at no charge.

What happens to patient data on the device?

It never leaves the device. We sign a business associate agreement with every account, and if you ask us to wipe stored records we log the wipe on the work order.

What if you cannot fix it?

You pay nothing beyond the free inspection. If a part has to be ordered just for you, we say so before ordering it.

Will a repair affect the maker's service contract?

We check first. If our work would void an active contract, we tell you and route the device to the maker instead.

What does the warranty cover?

The part we fitted and our work on it, for twelve months from the work order date. Drops and fluid ingress are not covered, but we will still inspect the device for free.

Do you buy retired equipment?

We do, for parts or to rebuild for the pre-owned shelf. We make an offer after inspection and give you a data-wipe certificate for every unit.
