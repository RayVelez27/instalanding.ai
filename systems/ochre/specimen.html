Six ways it meets you

Every plan includes all of them. Turn on what helps, ignore the rest — Ochre learns from what you actually use.

  • 01 Adaptive sessions Tell it how you feel in a sentence. It writes a session around that — length, pace, focus — and adjusts as you go. Every session · AI-built
  • 02 Breath sensing Hold your phone or wear earbuds. Ochre hears your breathing slow and paces the guidance to it — never ahead of you. Microphone · On device
  • 03 Sleep Stories that fade as your breathing deepens, and a soft alarm that wakes you in your lightest sleep. Nightly · 20–60 min
  • 04 Focus Fifty-minute blocks wrapped in a soundscape, with a two-minute reset between them so the afternoon doesn't eat you. Workdays · 50 min
  • 05 Daily check-in Thirty seconds each morning: one word for your mood, one for your energy. Ochre remembers so you don't have to. Mornings · 30 sec
  • 06 Weekly letter A short note each Sunday on your sleep, stress and practice — written in plain words, not dashboards. Sundays · In the app

16 · faq

FAQ

A sticky thin title beside native details rows between ink rules: the question in 300 with a ring button whose plus turns into a cross and whose ring turns sun when open.

Before you ask

Is my breathing recorded?

No. The microphone signal is analysed on your phone in real time and thrown away. Only your breaths per minute are kept, and only if you allow it.

How does the AI build a session?

Our teachers wrote thousands of short passages of guidance. Ochre's model arranges and paces them around what you tell it and how you're breathing, then reads them in a voice you choose.

Is this a replacement for therapy?

No. Ochre is a meditation companion, not a medical device. If you're having a hard time, it will point you to people who can help.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Download any session or soundscape. With no signal, adaptive sessions run on a smaller model that lives on your phone.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, in two taps from settings. Annual plans are refundable within thirty days, no questions asked.

Is it suitable for children?

The family plan includes a kids' mode for ages 6–12, with shorter sessions, bedtime stories and no journal or microphone features.
