03 · tiles

Geometric tiles

The system's one picture primitive: a square <i class="tk-t"> filled with --tk-tb, holding a <b> shape in --tk-tf turned by --tk-r quarter turns. Five shapes: tk-q (a quarter-round), tk-h (a half disc), tk-ring (a quarter ring), tk-dot (a centred disc) and tk-bar (a pill). Tiles build marks, mosaics, clusters and corners; any ink can be ground or shape.

04 · type

Type & echo

Gabarito set heavy and uppercase for display (900 at --tk-display, line-height .9), 800 for h3s and 700 for statements; Instrument Sans for body and leads. Words marked data-tk-echo get stacked outline copies underneath them (data-tk-echo-inks colours the copies pink, amber, light, ink), and .tk-fit words size themselves to fill their container from --tk-len, the letter count.

Rail steps

From brief to production

Every course follows the same twelve weeks, whatever you are building. Reviews every Thursday, AI audits every Saturday.

See the fees
  1. Weeks 1–2

    Audit

    A real product or a real cause. You inventory every colour, font and button before you design anything.

  2. Weeks 3–5

    Tokens

    Fifty names, pared down to a scale. We review them together and lock the foundations.

  3. Weeks 6–9

    Components & AI

    Components meet the tokens and models draft the variants. Weekly reviews keep them honest.

  4. Weeks 10–12

    Ship

    Docs, code sync and a release to a live product. Then demo day, and a glass of something.
