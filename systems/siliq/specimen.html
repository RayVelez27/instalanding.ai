12 · faq

FAQ

A sky side tile with a handwritten note beside white question tiles; the honey plus spins into a sky cross and the answer opens on its natural height.

Good questions

The things people ask at the front desk while their board is being flashed.

Still stuck? Ask Joe!

Ask us anything
Will it work with my ML framework?

Out of the box: PyTorch, ONNX and the common runtimes, with drivers pre-flashed and tested. Tell us your stack and we will check compatibility before you order.

How late can I order?

Until 9 pm for next-morning dispatch. Custom builds and anything laser-engraved need a week.

Do you deliver?

Our cargo bikes cover two miles around Foundry Row from 9 am, Tuesday to Saturday, and everything else ships overnight. Delivery is free for Compute club members and $9 for everyone else.

Can I order a custom build?

Yes, with a week's notice. Send us the workload, the budget and one wild idea; we will come back with a parts list and a price.

Are classes okay for complete beginners?

Absolutely. Most people at the bench have never held a soldering iron. You get safety glasses, a printed guide and as much patience as you need.
