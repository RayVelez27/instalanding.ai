03 · type

Type, numerals & labels

Everything is Oswald. Display type is uppercase, heavy and tight: section titles at --mu-display-section (600, .92 line height, -3px) with the second line in the moving --mu-holo foil, and the hero's index numerals at --mu-display-numeral in the same foil with a soft rose glow (.mu-holo-num). Small labels are uppercase Oswald 400-600 at 13-16px; .mu-section-num puts a 20x3px ink dash before an index (02 — Lab). Statements are big and light (300) with the claim in 600.

05 · swarm

Sphere swarm

The page's three.js cluster rebuilt in CSS: the scene's own hundred-odd spheres projected through its camera into a box with the cluster's aspect ratio, each an <i> with the --mu-sphere-fill radial gradient placed by --mu-x/--mu-y and sized by --mu-s, drawn back to front under one soft --mu-shadow-swarm. They fly up into place (--mu-dur-swarm-in, staggered by --mu-swarm-stagger), breathe up and down by --mu-breathe, and a fine pointer pushes the nearest ones away (--mu-push) before they ease back. Off screen the breathing pauses; under reduced motion it is still. Add .mu-gold to an <i> for a gold sphere.

06 · stub

Admission stub & barcode

The small ticket from the hero (.mu-ticket.mu-wide.mu-hero-ticket): a leaf-shaped ink tag (--mu-radius-tag, 999px 0), a title in Oswald 700 and an "Admit one" line on foil, a dashed tear, and a stub with a serial number and a CSS barcode (22 box-shadow bars in ink) over its code. Notches sit either side of the tear (--mu-ticket-notch), the outer edges are perforated (--mu-ticket-perf). Status lines put a ringed dot before an uppercase state: rose for beta and preview, --mu-live for generally available.

08 · products

Product tickets

The products as a grid of three vertical holographic tickets, the middle column dropped 90px. Each ticket is a die-cut foil card (.mu-ticket-bg: a conic gradient of --mu-foil-* under drifting iridescent rings and a pointer-following sheen, cut by an intersecting mask into perforated ends and two side notches above the stub). Inside: a leaf tag and serial (No. 01 ✦), a big uppercase title, a cluster of spheres that spread on hover, a dashed tear, and the stub with a one-line description, a status and a barcode. A huge white watermark numeral sits behind in overlay. Tickets float on --mu-dur-float, out of phase by --mu-i, and tilt toward a fine pointer.

The
Lab

02 — Lab

Murmur Labs is an applied AI research lab. We believe intelligence isn't one giant model — it's hundreds of specialised agents colliding, challenging each other and settling on answers you can trust.

Every sphere above is an agent. Swarm, our platform, lets teams spin up, coordinate and evaluate agent collectives in production — with the rigour of a research lab and the reliability of infrastructure.

11 · models

Model family

Six tall tickets in a row, each its own foil (the ticket's --mu-holo-1 to --mu-holo-7 set inline) with one sphere sized to the model. Hovering one widens it (flex 1 to 2.4 over --mu-dur-open), opens its description, grows its sphere and slides in its barcode.
