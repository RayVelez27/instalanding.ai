Nimbus Design system · nimbus@1.0.0 · 15 components

Soft sky, one coral, and phones that float.

A light consumer-app launch language: a pale grey page with white 22px cards and a whisper of shadow, one coral accent used as tints and tags, Instrument Serif display over Inter, a canvas cloud sky, two tilted phones drawn in CSS, and tool tiles orbiting the logo. Extracted from the Nimbus AI Assistant page.

01 · nav

Glass pill nav

A fixed glass pill (dark translucent, 22px blur at 170% saturation, a 1px gradient rim): the logo, five links under a sliding highlight that follows the pointer and the active section, and a white Get started pill with a black arrow disc that turns -45deg on hover. .nb-scrolled narrows and darkens it. Under 1200px the links become a round menu button that opens a dark panel of big numbered serif links with arrow discs and a green All systems normal label.

02 · buttons

Buttons, pills & tags

Buttons are 10px-radius blocks with the soft shadow that lift 2px on hover: ink (.nb-btn-dark, with an icon) and white (.nb-btn-light). On dark panels the action is a white pill with a black arrow disc (.nb-nav-cta) beside a glass ghost pill. Carousel controls are white 40px squares and dots that stretch to an ink bar; the billing switch is a pill with a grey track that turns coral. Social links are glass discs that fill white; the status pill is mint on green.

03 · type

Serif display & coral tags

Headings are Instrument Serif 400 with tight tracking (-.03em): h1 at --nb-h1, h2 at --nb-h2, both stepping down at 1199 and 809px. Everything else is Inter 500; h3 and h4 stay in Inter. Every section title sits under a small coral tag with an icon: 8% coral fill, a 16% coral rim, 13px Inter 600. .nb-plain is a grey label tag, .nb-soft a borderless coral one.

04 · sky

Cloud sky

The signature: a sky gradient (--nb-sky-top to --nb-sky-bottom) with clouds drawn on canvas. One radial puff is stamped 20-90 times, rotated and scaled, into each cloud; clouds are spread by best-candidate sampling over faint stars, sorted by depth, drift left and wrap, and parallax with the section's scroll offset through a damped follow. It only animates while on screen; under reduced motion it draws once.

05 · hero

Sky hero & phones

A 10px-radius card filled with the cloud sky under the glass nav: a white logo badge in a glass frame, the serif h1 in white with a soft blue text shadow, one line of copy, ink and white buttons, then two phones tilted by ±--nb-tilt and cropped by the card's bottom edge.

How does the AI app work?

The app analyzes your inputs, understands your intent, and generates accurate responses or actions using its built-in AI models. It adapts over time based on how you use it.

Can the AI app learn my preferences?

Yes. The AI can remember your preferences, tone, and workflow (if memory features are enabled), helping it provide more personalized and relevant responses over time.

What kind of tasks does the AI app handle?

It specializes in writing, content creation, automation, data assistance, idea generation, and everyday productivity tasks. You can ask it to draft, explain, summarize, or create almost anything.

Is my data secure when using the AI app?

Yes. User data is protected with industry-standard security measures, and privacy controls help ensure your information remains safe and confidential.

Does the AI app offer ongoing support?

Yes. The app automatically updates with new features, improved models, and performance enhancements as they become available.

How long does the app take to respond?

Most responses are generated within seconds. More complex tasks may take slightly longer, but the app is optimized for fast output.

Can the AI app integrate with other tools?

Depending on the version, the app can connect with external platforms, export outputs, and support API-based integrations for a smoother workflow.
