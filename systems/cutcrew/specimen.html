The Cutcrew Design System.

Candy-coloured robot mascots, grey trays holding white cards, Switzer set large and tight and one electric green. Extracted from the Cutcrew AI video agents page.

01 · buttons

Buttons & chips

Buttons are 10px-radius blocks, not pills: dark (the default action), grey (secondary), white and electric green (on dark or colour), and a small dark one for the nav. Every button lifts 1px on hover and picks up a green glow (--cc-glow). Chips: the dark eyebrow square with a white line icon and a moulded --cc-shadow-chip, and the pale-green status pill with a green dot.

02 · type

Type & statement

Switzer at weight 400, set large and tight (h1 104px down to h6 20px, -.03em to -.01em tracking), with grey copy at 60% ink. The statement line sets 60px words with the mascots dropped in as tilted 58px slots, the tail fading to 35% ink.

The five agents, Cut, Hue, Nova, Echo and Flow, as candy-coloured robots on their own tinted tiles, each with its own eyes, mouth and prop (antenna, droplet, sparkle, headphones, squiggle) and a levitation trail; they float on SMIL and bob in white-framed tiles over a faded phone.
