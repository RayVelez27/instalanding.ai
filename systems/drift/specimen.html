02 · buttons

Buttons, presets & status chips

One drawn object per screen: the primary is a cream pill (.dr-pill-cta, or .dr-dl.dr-dl--mac with an icon) and the solid keyed button (.dr-btn.dr-btn--solid), whose 2px frame has three gaps punched in --dr-key-bg and a dash leading the label; on hover it empties to an outline and the gaps close. Secondary actions are hairlines (.dr-btn, .dr-dl--win). Presets are square mono toggles that fill cream when on (aria-pressed). Status chips carry a dot and a word, tinted by --dr-s: on track, fetching, solving, pushing, hold.

03 · type

04 · hero

Wind field hero

The page's signature: a live wind field behind everything. Particles advect through the sum of six pressure vortices and leave fading trails coloured by speed on the wind ramp (--dr-wind-1 calm to --dr-wind-8 gale); over them, isobars every 4 hPa contoured from the same pressure, L and H chips that follow the systems, the great circle dashed and the cheapest track solid with a ship on it. The headline sits on top in Archivo capitals with a soft --dr-shadow-headline, a live slot of the field set between its words, and the chart's furniture frames it: caption and route readout up top, legend and preset row below. The six presets swap the systems; the route card re-solves. Canvas 2D, DPR 1, paused off screen, one held frame under reduced motion.

08 · bridge

Advisory dial

The routing advisory as an instrument: three rings (waypoint, a full compass tape, arrival day) turn on --dr-ease-dial to bring the current value under the index mark; the centre is a compass card with the course hand, the wind hand in --dr-low and the solver's blue sweep; two side dials show the wind at the waypoint and a bar per waypoint along the track, coloured on the wind ramp with numbers under every bar. A mono readout and seven waypoint buttons (aria-pressed) sit below. It steps through the voyage by itself while on screen and stops for 15s when someone picks a waypoint.

One page. Every waypoint.

Each solve prints to a single advisory sheet sized for a 40 kB attachment over Inmarsat. Positions, courses and speeds the officer of the watch can steer, with the wind and pressure the solver saw at each one.

12 · access

FAQ & sign-up

A section title and lede over a one-line sign-up (a hairline email field and the solid keyed button), with a polite status line; beside it a <details> accordion ruled top and bottom, its mono + turning to a cream dash when open. The demo form validates natively and sends nothing.

Put one hull on the field

We open a berth a week. Tell us the vessel and we'll route the next leg alongside your current provider, then show you both tracks.

Where does the field come from?

Global pressure and wind at 0.25°, re-gridded onto our own vortex model every six minutes. The chart on this page runs the same model at demo scale, so you can see what the solver sees.

Is this a forecast or a routing advice?

Both, and neither is the deliverable. The deliverable is a track: waypoints and speeds your bridge can steer, with the field that produced them attached so a master can argue with it.

How does it reach the bridge?

API, email, or the one-page advisory sheet over Inmarsat, which fits in a 40 kB attachment. The board on this page is the same data, rendered for the office.

What happens when the field is wrong?

Every solve is stored with the field it used. When a system deepens faster than modelled, the re-solve arrives with a diff instead of a new PDF that quietly replaces the old one.
