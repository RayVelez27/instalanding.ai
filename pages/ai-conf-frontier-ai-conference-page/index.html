[FAQ]

Questions, Answered

Everything you need to plan your two days at AI/CONF.

Do I need a machine learning background?

Not for every session. Each talk is tagged Intro, Practitioner or Deep Dive, so product leaders and seasoned researchers can both build a schedule that fits.

What do I need for the build labs?

Just a laptop. Pro and Founders Circle passes include cloud GPU credits and pre-configured environments, so you can start building in minutes.

Will sessions be recorded?

All main-stage talks are recorded and available to every pass holder within two weeks. Research salons are off the record.

Can I submit a talk or poster?

Yes — the call for proposals is open until 15 Dec 2026. Use the contact form below and choose “Speaking & Research Posters”.

Are there student or academic discounts?

We reserve discounted passes for students and university researchers. Reach out with your institutional email to request one.

What is the refund policy?

Full refunds up to 30 days before the event. After that, passes can be transferred to a teammate at no cost.
