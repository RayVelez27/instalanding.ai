Questions

Before you begin.

What do I need to get started?

Your date, time and place of birth. If you don’t know your birth time, Sidera still works — it simply leaves out the house-based insights until you add it.

How is this different from a horoscope?

A horoscope is written for a twelfth of the world. Sidera reads your full chart against the live sky, so two people with the same sun sign receive very different guidance.

Does the AI make predictions?

No. Sidera describes the astrological climate and offers reflection prompts and timing windows. Decisions — and their outcomes — remain entirely yours.

What happens to my birth data?

It’s encrypted at rest and in transit, used only to calculate your readings, never sold or shared, and you can delete it at any time.
