Questions before bedtime

What happens when an agent gets stuck?+

It writes down what it tried, parks that piece of work, and moves on to the rest. You'll see it at the top of the morning digest with everything you need to unblock it.

Can it do anything irreversible without me?+

Only if you say so. Sending, paying and deleting are checkpoints by default, and you choose which apps and actions each brief can use.

Where does my data go?+

Runs execute in an isolated workspace, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and nothing you connect is used to train models. Enterprise plans can pin data to a region.
