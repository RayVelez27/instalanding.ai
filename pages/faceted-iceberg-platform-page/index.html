    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Can't find what you need? Our solutions team replies within one business day.

    How is Halcyra different from calling a model API directly?

    A model API gives you raw intelligence. Halcyra gives you everything around it — retrieval over your data, tool use, durable workflows, memory, evals, guardrails and observability — so you ship agents instead of plumbing.

    Is our data used to train models?

    Never. Your data stays in your workspace, is encrypted at rest and in transit, and is never used to train shared models. Enterprise plans can pin data to a region or run fully in your own cloud.

    Which models can we use?

    Use our tuned defaults or bring your own keys for any major provider or open-weight model. You can route per task — a fast model for triage, a stronger one for reasoning — and swap without rewriting agents.

    How long does it take to go live?

    Most teams connect their first sources in an afternoon and have an agent in production within a week. Enterprise rollouts with SSO and custom deployment typically take two to four weeks.

    Can we self-host Halcyra?

    Yes. Enterprise customers can deploy into their own VPC or on-prem environment with the same platform, updates and support as our cloud.
