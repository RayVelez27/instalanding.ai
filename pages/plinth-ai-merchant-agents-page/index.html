Four stages, never rushed. Each one ends with something you can keep, even if you never turn the agents fully loose.

  1. Connect

    We connect your stack in a day

    Storefront, helpdesk, warehouse and payments, read-only first. Nothing changes in your store until you say so.

  2. Train

    Agents learn your store, not the internet

    Policies, past tickets and your brand voice, studied in a week. You read their practice answers before anyone else does.

  3. Launch

    We launch in shadow, then go live

    Agents draft and people approve for the first fortnight. Each one goes live on its own, only when its numbers say it’s ready.

  4. Scale

    We stay through your biggest night

    A launch engineer on call for your first peak season, weekly reviews of every agent, and new agents when you’re ready for them.
