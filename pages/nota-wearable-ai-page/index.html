How it works

Press, speak, let go. That's the whole interface.

There's no wake word and no app to open. NOTA only listens while your thumb is on the key.

STEP 1 · HOLD
ASK

Hold the key

The LED turns orange and the microphones open. Let go and they close again.

STEP 2 · SPEAK

Say it plainly

Ask about anything said near you in the last 90 days, or ask it to remember something.

STEP 3 · READ
NOTA › Thursday 3:40 PM. Dr. Okafor moved it this morning.

Read or listen

The answer appears on the display and plays quietly from the 12 mm speaker. Turn the crown to Quiet to silence it.

Privacy

The light tells everyone what it's doing.

The LED on the face is wired to the microphones' power. People around you can see whether NOTA is listening, and it can't listen without that light.

OffMic switch open. Microphones have no power.
WhiteReady. Nothing is being heard.
OrangeListening while you hold the key.
PinkSaving a note you asked for.
BlueTranslating the person in front of you.
Crown positions

Four modes, one turn apart.

Each crown position changes what the ask key does. The display shows a different view for each one.

Build

Machined titanium and sapphire glass.

Each body is cut from Grade 5 titanium over 41 minutes, then bead-blasted to match the grey of the face. The name is pressed into the back.
