How the swarm works

Four agents,
one shift.

01
01

Plan

The Scheduler agent reads check-outs, bookings and who is on shift, then builds every route before the first coffee. Late check-out? It reshuffles in seconds.

02
02

Stock

The Supply agent counts what each room used and reorders linen, amenities and chemicals before the shelf runs bare. No forms. One tap to approve.

03
03

Answer

The Concierge agent replies to guests and clients — door codes, extra towels, "can you come Thursday?" — and only pings you when it truly needs a person.

04
04

Check

The Inspector agent reviews a quick photo of each finished room against your standard. Housekeepers see what to fix; supervisors see the trend.
