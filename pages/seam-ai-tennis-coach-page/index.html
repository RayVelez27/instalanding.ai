Why Seam

Every shot you've ever hit had exactly one witness that saw all of it: the ball. Seam gives that witness a voice. It reads spin off the seam, finds the strings you actually hit, follows your feet between shots, and turns every session into one clear thing to fix next.

No sensors · No wearables · Just your phone and the court

FAQ

Questions,
returned.

Do I need sensors or special balls?

No. Seam uses only your phone's camera. Put it on the fence mount behind the baseline, or on any tripod, and play with your usual racket and balls.

Which phones does it work on?

Recent iPhones and flagship Android phones that can record at 240 fps. On slower cameras Seam still works, but its spin readings are less precise.

Hard court, clay, grass, indoors?

All of them. Seam finds the lines on any surface and adjusts its bounce model for each one, so a kick serve on clay is measured as a kick serve on clay.

Where does my video go?

It stays on your phone. All of the analysis runs on the device, and video is uploaded only when you choose to share a clip or session.

Can my coach use it with me?

Yes. Invite your coach to any session. They see every shot, can comment on specific moments, and can assign drills that appear in your plan.
