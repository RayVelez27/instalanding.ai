Before you ask

Still unsure? Write to us and an agronomist will answer.

Who owns my data?

You do. Every image, reading and map can be exported at any time, and we never sell or share field data. Models trained on your farm stay yours.

Does it work without mobile coverage?

Yes. Probes talk to the mast over LoRa up to 12 km, and the mast buffers a week of data. The drone processes its flight on the dock and syncs when it can.

Will it work with my machinery?

Prescription maps export as ISOXML and shapefiles, which every major sprayer and spreader controller reads. Pivot control works with most panels made after 2005.

Does the AI make decisions on its own?

Only the ones you allow. By default every action waits for a tap on your phone. Many growers let Hydra run irrigation on its own after the first month.

Where do you operate?

Monitoring works anywhere. Hardware installation and field engineers are available across North America, the UK and the EU, with partners in Australia and Southeast Asia.
