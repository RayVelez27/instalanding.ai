Questions

Before you
commit (loosely).

Everything you wanted to know about adopting an idea you won’t finish.

Can I return an idea?

Of course. Hit “release idea” and it goes right back to the board. Ideas don’t hold grudges. Mostly.

Do I actually have to finish it?

Absolutely not. Adopting an idea is not a legally binding commitment to finish it. We checked. Sort of.

What if someone else adopts the same idea?

Ideas are infinite. That’s the whole problem. Everyone can have their own half-built to-do app.

Is my adoption saved anywhere?

Nope. Adoptions live in this page’s memory only. Nothing is transmitted or stored, so a refresh sets every idea free again.

Can I submit my own idea?

You already have twelve in your notes app. Maybe start one of those first.
