Fair questions.

Does Easel train on my work?

Only if you switch on a private style model, and then only for your account. Nothing you upload is used to train shared models.

Who owns what I make?

You do. Suggestions arrive as layers in your own file, and everything you export is yours to sell, print or licence.

Will it imitate other artists?

No. Easel declines requests that name living artists, and its base model is trained on licensed and public-domain images.

Which files can it open and export?

PSD with named layers, PNG, JPG, TIFF, SVG and print-ready PDF. Layers come back exactly as you left them.

Does it work with my tablet?

Yes. Pressure, tilt and rotation pass straight through; Easel only listens when you ask it something.

Can I use it offline?

Sketching and layer tools work offline. Suggestions need a connection and sync when you are back.
