We teach a machine two centuries of tailoring. Then a tailor gets the last word.

Latent began in 2021 as three engineers and a cutter sharing a basement and a single GPU. Today the atelier drafts every pattern from a 3D scan of the person who will wear it, tests the drape in simulation, then cuts, bonds and finishes each piece by hand in the room behind the shop.

Process · 9 days

From scan to shoulder

Nine days from the booth to your door, with a person checking every step the machine takes.

Book a scan

  1. 01 / 04

    Scan

    Stand in the booth for twelve seconds. We capture 200,000 points and keep only the measurements your patterns need.

  2. 02 / 04

    Generate

    The pattern model drafts your piece and simulates the drape on your body. You watch it move before you approve it.

  3. 03 / 04

    Cut

    Panels are laser-cut from the roll, then bonded, stitched and pressed by hand in the atelier.

  4. 04 / 04

    Wear

    Collect it in the shop or have it couriered. Re-fits are free for the first year, because bodies change.

See the wall in the atelier

Asked at the booth

Anything else? Ask us in the atelier, or write and a person replies within a day.

What happens to my body scan?

We keep the measurements your patterns need and nothing else. The raw point cloud is deleted after your fitting, and the measurements go too the moment you ask.

Does the model design everything?

It drafts and grades patterns. People choose the fabrics, set the silhouettes and sign off every piece before it's cut.

How long does made to measure take?

Nine working days on average. Shell outerwear can take up to fourteen when the bonding queue is long.

Can I return something made for me?

Within 14 days for a free re-fit, or store credit if it still isn't right. Every seam is guaranteed for two years.

How do I wash the Lumen pieces?

Unclip the battery, then hand-wash cold. The fibres are rated for two hundred washes before they start to dim.

Do you ship?

Ready-to-wear ships worldwide. Made to measure needs one scan in person; after that, re-orders can be placed from home.
