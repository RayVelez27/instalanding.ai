(06) FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Landra generate a page?

Landra reads your prompt (and your site or brand kit if you share one), plans the sections your audience needs, writes the copy, picks a layout and type system, generates visuals and assembles it all into a responsive, production-ready page.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. Ask for changes in plain language — “shorter headline”, “add testimonials”, “make it darker” — or click any element to edit it directly. Every change is versioned so you can roll back.

Can I use my own domain?

On Pro and Team plans you can connect any domain in a couple of clicks. SSL, CDN hosting and caching are included.

Do I own the code?

Completely. Export clean HTML/CSS or React components at any time and host them wherever you like — no lock-in.

Will it match my brand?

Paste your website URL or upload a logo and Landra builds a brand kit with your colors, fonts and voice, then applies it to every page you generate.

Is there a free plan?

Yes — Starter is free forever with three generated pages a month on a landra.site subdomain. Upgrade whenever you need more.
