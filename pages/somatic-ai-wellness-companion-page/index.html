How we build

Four promises, written before the first line of code. Every feature still has to pass all four.

  1. Private

    Your raw data stays encrypted and most of the thinking happens on your phone. Never sold. Ever.

  2. Evidence

    Every suggestion traces back to peer-reviewed research, checked by our clinical advisors.

  3. No guilt

    No streaks to break, no red warnings. A bad night is information, not a failure.

  4. Human loop

    When a pattern looks worth checking, Somatic says so and points you to a real clinician.
