Nine days, planned in ninety seconds

A real trip from the beta, as Wayfind ran it. The plan bent twice along the way, and the traveller found out about it afterwards.

  1. Day 1

    Landing

    The rental car is waiting, the route is on the phone and the first night is somewhere you'd never have found yourself.

  2. Days 2–3

    Off the list

    Scout swaps the famous viewpoint for one locals use, timed for the hour when nobody else is there.

  3. Day 4

    The storm

    A sandstorm closes the road. Fixer moves the camp, the dinner and the next two nights before breakfast is over.

  4. Days 5–6

    No signal

    Two hundred kilometres without coverage. Compass already has the maps, the fuel stops and the phrasebook offline.

  5. Day 7

    Nothing booked

    A day left empty on purpose. Wayfind only speaks up if you ask, or if the stars are going to be unusually good.

  6. Days 8–9

    Home, sorted

    A last swim, a flight upgraded with points, and Ledger has already split the bills between four friends.

Before you ask

Anything missing? Ask Wayfind itself, or write to us and a real person answers within a day.

Write to us
Does it book things without asking me?

Only if you let it. By default Wayfind proposes and you approve. You can give it a spending limit for rebookings, and it will always ask before going over.

What happens to my data?

Your trips and preferences are encrypted and never sold. You can export or delete everything from settings at any time.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Maps, tickets, translations and your full plan are downloaded before you leave, and Compass keeps working offline.

Can I plan with friends or family?

Invite anyone to a trip. Wayfind balances everyone's wishes, and Ledger keeps track of who paid for what.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Every pick is checked against live availability and our local experts' notes. If something still goes wrong, a human on our team steps in and makes it right.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in two taps; your past trips stay yours, and yearly plans are refunded for unused months.
