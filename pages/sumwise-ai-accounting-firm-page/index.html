Questions people ask before letting an AI do their books.

Anything else, ask Sumwise itself during the free trial, or ask a human on a demo call.

Does an AI really file my taxes?+

Sumwise prepares everything, and a licensed CPA on our team reviews and signs every return before it's filed. You see it and approve it on your phone first.

Is my financial data safe?+

Bank connections are read-only, and your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. It's never used to train models for anyone else, and you can export or delete it at any time.

What if it gets something wrong?+

When it's unsure about something, it asks a human instead of guessing. If we make a mistake on a filing, we fix it and pay any penalties and interest it caused.

Which banks and software does it work with?+

Most US banks and card issuers, plus Stripe, Square, Shopify and PayPal. If you already use QuickBooks or Xero, it imports your history in one go.

Can I talk to a person?+

Always. Every plan includes a named CPA you can reach by chat, email or video, and the Scale plan adds a monthly call.

How easy is it to leave?+

Cancel in any month, with no exit fee. Export your full books, documents and filings in one click, in formats any accountant can read.
