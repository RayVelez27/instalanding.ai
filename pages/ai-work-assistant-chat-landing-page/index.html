Questions, answered.

Does Tidewell send anything without asking me?

No. Tidewell drafts, sorts and suggests. Anything that leaves your account, like a sent reply or a calendar invite, waits for your approval unless you turn on auto-send for a specific rule.

Is my email used to train AI models?

Never. Your messages are encrypted in transit and at rest, processed only to serve your requests, and you can delete everything Tidewell has stored with one click.

Which email providers work?

Every major work email provider, plus IMAP in beta on the Pro and Team plans.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from settings and you keep access until the end of your billing period. Your inbox stays exactly as you left it.
